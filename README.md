# Morph: Revived (Fabric/NeoForge)

### **What is this mod?**

*   This this a partial rewrite/fork for iChun's Morph Mod for new versions! If your like me an played iChun's Morph mod back in 1.12.2 and haven't found anything to scratch that itch since, here's the solution! (Original Mod: [https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/morph](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/morph))
*   This mod take iChun's Morph mod and brings it to the newest versions of MC while also adding some features and completing some unfinished features!

### **Previously Unfinished Features  
**

*   **Abilities Implementation** - Abilities weren't quite fleshed out enough and a lot of mobs had similar or lackluster abilities. Those have now been fleshed out to include new mob interactions and Special Abilities for special mob attacks :)  
       --(Special) Sonic Screech: Warden can Right-Click to perform screech attack  
      --(Special) Dragon's Breath: As the Ender Dragon shoot it's destructive purple fireball  
      --(Special) Wither Skull: As the Wither shoot Wither Skulls  
      --(Special) Roll Up: As the Armadillo Roll Up for 5 seconds and take 50% less damage  
      --(Special) Detonate: As the Creeper..... Explode.  
      --(Passive) Dolphin's Grace: As the Dolphin give nearby swimmers Dolphin's Grace  
      --(Passive) Dolphin's Leap: As the Dolphin leap out of the water  
      --(Passive) Darkness Aura: Warden give surrounding Players Darkness effect  
      --(Trait) Emissive: Mobs give off light  
      --(Trait) Can Be Bucketed: Can be put in a Bucket  
      --(Trait) Shearable: Can be sheared  
      --(Trait) Full Bright: Gives night vision effect  
      
    
*   **Variations and Babies**  \--Variations of all animals and all babies are stacked on top of adult animals in GUI to reduce clutter
*   **Biomass Progression** \- iChun had partially written in a sort of _Progression System_, that is now fully implemented!  
      --**Biomass**: Biomass is a secondary type of XP tied to your Morph. The more Health/Mass a mob has the more Biomass you recieve from killing that mob  
      --**Biomass Upgrades**: You can use _Biomass_ to purchase upgrades to use _Abilities  
    _  --**Progression**: Biomass is now needed to unlock Morphing and Use of Abilities
